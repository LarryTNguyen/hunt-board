The field method

One route from sighting to signal.

The workflow and the safeguards belong together. Hunt Board scouts, verifies, and ranks each role while keeping the evidence, rules, and run history visible.

Scout / ATS01

Watch the right ground.

Pull fresh roles directly from curated Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby boards—on your schedule, from sources you chose.

Verify / Identity02

Keep the trail honest.

Normalize every record, retain the raw source, detect clear duplicates, and flag uncertain matches instead of erasing evidence.

Rank / Preference03

Surface your signal.

Score roles by title relevance, level, freshness, location, work style, and source priority—then explain what rose to the top.

Field rule / evidence

Retain the source.

Every normalized posting keeps its latest raw payload and readable description.

Field rule / identity

Never erase uncertainty.

Closed roles become inactive. Possible duplicates wait for review.

Field rule / preference

Your rules come first.

Exact phrases stay strict while flexible role groups widen only useful territory.

Field rule / observability

Every run leaves tracks.

Source metrics show what was fetched, changed, skipped, or failed.
